Scroll the page. Whatever is wrapped moves more slowly than the block around it, so it reads as further away. It works on a picture, a pattern, or one of each at different depths. The browser does it from the scroll position alone: there is no script, and in a browser without scroll-driven animation the background simply stays put. Anyone who has asked their system to reduce motion gets the same picture, holding still.
