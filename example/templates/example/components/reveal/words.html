Scroll the page. The paragraph starts dim and each word lights as the page moves, so the reader is led through it at the pace they scroll. It suits one statement on a page, not body copy. While a word is dim it is fainter than body text should be, which is the effect and the reason to keep it to short, large type. The movement is tied to the scroll position, so it needs no script and it runs backwards when you scroll back up. In a browser without scroll-driven animation, and for anyone who has asked their system to reduce motion, the paragraph is at full strength.
